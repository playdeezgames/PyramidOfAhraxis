# Pyramid of Ahraxis comes back, in your browser

Title: Pyramid of Ahraxis is back: a new browser build

The old TIC-80 cart from September 2023 has been rebuilt for the browser. The rules and the map are the same as the original: keys open doors, potions heal, shields turn a blow aside, swords kill monsters, jools and the rest feed the score at the exit, and some floor tiles are traps.

What is new in this Metaphor:

- It is now written in Odin and compiled to WebAssembly, so it runs straight on the page (and on a phone: swipe to move).
- The pictures are redrawn with the Urizen 1-bit tileset (CC0, by vurmux) and the text uses the DINOBYTE font (CC BY 4.0, by mby). The view is wider, health is hearts, and messages float up from the action.
- New sounds, made with the Anvil of SPLORR!!, play for pickups, hits, traps and the exit.
- P drinks a potion now (it was Q in the cart). F2 still resets.

You can also play it at https://playdeezgames.github.io/PyramidOfAhraxis/ and the source is at https://github.com/playdeezgames/PyramidOfAhraxis. The old Windows, Linux and Mac downloads of the original cart are still on the page.
